Push notifications (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) — notify both of you when the other adds/checks off a grocery item or todo, so you don't have to keep the app open to stay in sync. Natural next step given the PWA work already done.
Expense splitting / shared bills — who paid for what (groceries, rent, utilities), running balance of who owes whom. Very common pain point for couples living together.
Chore rotation/schedule — recurring tasks (trash, dishes, cleaning) with whose-turn-is-it tracking, instead of one-off todos.
Meal planning — a simple weekly menu that can push its ingredients straight into the Bevás list.
Recurring todos — some of your "change lightbulb" style todos might really be one-offs, but chores like "water the plants" recur — worth a repeat-every-N-days option instead of manually re-adding.
